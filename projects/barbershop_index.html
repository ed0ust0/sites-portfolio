A barbershop built on time well spent

Hartley & Vane opened in 2012 in a converted ironmonger's on Sallow Street. We kept the brick, the tiled floor and the habit of doing things properly.

We book every client for a full slot, so nobody is rushed and nobody is kept waiting. Each cut starts with a conversation about how you wear your hair, how often you visit and what your mornings look like. Then we cut for the way it will look in three weeks, not just when you leave the chair.

Three chairs
Never more than three clients in the shop at once.
Full slots
30 to 75 minutes per appointment, no double booking.
Walk-ins welcome
When a chair is free. Booking guarantees your time.
